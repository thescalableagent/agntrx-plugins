# AGNTRX™

AGNTRX™ by The Scalable Agent. Fourteen helpers for your real estate business: setup, your market, your design, your market numbers, your plan and your week, your weekly review, content, lead magnets, prospecting, follow up, Meta ads, YouTube, and appointment prep. Talk the way you already talk, and the right helper picks it up.

## Connect it
Needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).
1. Add the plugin.
2. Open the plugin's Connectors tab and click Connect.
3. Sign in with the 6-digit code we email you.
4. Click Connect.

## What it sends
Only your sign-in and the name of the helper you're using. Never your files, your notes, or any client's name, address, or price. Your hub stays on your computer, and everything you make is yours.

## If something's off
"AGNTRX isn't connected yet" means connect it from the Connectors tab. "Can't reach its home base" means try again in a few minutes. Say "check my hub" to see your plugin and home base versions.
